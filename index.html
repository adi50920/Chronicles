Chronicler.

Your life, remembered. Your patterns, understood.

A working prototype of the Chronicler mobile app — a personal journal that makes capturing your day effortless and lets you ask questions about your own life. Everything on the phone is clickable. The entries belong to a fictional user, Shubham.

Try this path

  1. Tap the splash screen, then Start my journal.
  2. Tap the green mic, let it record a few seconds, press the tick, then Done. Chronicler replies to what you said and asks how today feels.
  3. Open Calendar. Colours are the day tones you set, never guessed. Tap a day, then its card. Try Year and All time.
  4. Open Ask me and pick a question. Each answer separates what you wrote, what repeats, and a labelled guess, with source chips that open the original entries.
  5. Open My circle → Family → Share. Choose a tone, a few words and who sees it.

Feedback we're looking for

  • Does Today make you want to record something right away?
  • Does the AI's reply after you save feel helpful, or intrusive?
  • Are the calendar's day-tone colours easy to read?
  • Does My Circle feel private enough to share how you're really doing?
